Custom Vehicle General Guidelines (for developers)

1. Lore-Friendliness More info ↗

  • All real-world branding and badges must be removed.
  • A lore-friendly brand and name must be used.
  • Signature visual features must be altered from the original design.
  • Vehicles must not appear as direct replicas of real-world vehicles.

2. Optimization More info ↗

  • Maximum file size: 32 MiB per .yft and .ytd.
  • Maximum livery resolution: 2048 × 2048.
  • Maximum polygon count: 400,000.
  • Proper LODs are mandatory (minimum of L0, L1 and L2, each optimized for the respective distances).
  • Unnecessary metadata, geometry, materials, and textures must be removed.

3. Collision More info ↗

  • Collision must closely match the vehicle mesh.
  • Major impact areas must use flat, straight collision surfaces.
  • Low LOD collision must not exceed the door sill.
  • Bulletproof tyres are prohibited.
  • Unrealistic extensions with collisions are prohibited.

4. Functionality More info ↗

  • All vehicle parts must function correctly.
  • No floating, detached, or improperly rigged parts.

5. Meta Files More info ↗

  • Only standard gameplay flags are permitted.
  • Vehicles must use proper GTA layouts.
  • Maximum LOD distance: 350.0.
  • Server-provided handling must be used as the base.
  • Custom engine sounds may be used; however, extremely loud sounds are prohibited.

6. Hierarchy & Nodes More info ↗

  • Vehicles must follow Rockstar's hierarchy structure.
  • All required nodes and bones must be correctly positioned.
  • Flying and submersible vehicles are not permitted.

7. Appearance More info ↗

  • Glow effects limited to 30%.
  • All animations are prohibited.
Final Approval Any vehicle may be rejected if it causes performance issues, contains major visual defects, provides an unfair gameplay advantage, or fails to meet the above standards.
